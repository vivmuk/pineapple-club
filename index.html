— about us —

Why the pineapple? 🍍 — and why upside down?

The pineapple has long been the hospitality symbol — a welcome sign for the open-minded. Displayed upside down, it becomes a quiet, knowing signal: "we're lifestyle-friendly." Our club borrows that spirit: warm welcomes, zero pressure, and total discretion. We host socials, meetups, and themed nights where like-minded adults can connect at their own pace.

🤝

Vetted & Verified

Every member is screened by our host team. No creeps, no pressure — a community built on trust.

🤐

Discreet by Default

What happens at the club stays at the club. No photos without consent, ever. Real names stay optional.

🎉

Social First

Icebreakers, games, themed parties and resort weekends. Chemistry happens naturally — no awkwardness.

🛡️

Safe & Sober-Friendly

Consent ambassadors at every event, clear boundaries, and a strict no means no policy.

— house rules —

The Golden Rules

1️⃣
Enthusiastic consent, always. A yes means yes. A maybe means no. No exceptions.
2️⃣
No means no — the first time. Never ask twice. Never negotiate.
3️⃣
Couples decide together. We respect the pair-bond above all else.
4️⃣
Discretion is sacred. Outing anyone in the lifestyle is grounds for a permanent ban.
5️⃣
Be the guest you'd want to host. Kindness, hygiene, and self-awareness go a long way.
